## 2. Classification Scheme

Each candidate artifact was assigned one of five strict classifications:
1. **REMOVE:** Conclusively obsolete, redundant, unreferenced, or safely regenerable build caches.
2. **CONSOLIDATE:** Duplicates another implementation; migrated to a single canonical location before deletion.
3. **ARCHIVE:** Historically valuable but no longer active; preserved in canonical `archive/` or dedicated reports.
4. **KEEP:** Actively used, necessary, or protected under non-negotiable constraints (model weights, legal registers, benchmarks).
5. **INVESTIGATE:** Insufficient evidence to safely decide (0 items).
