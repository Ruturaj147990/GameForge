# GameForge 1.0 — Component Improvement & Re-Engineering Coverage Ledger

This ledger tracks every project-owned file, module, and component in the GameForge IDE.
Under the mandatory component-by-component improvement protocol, **every component must be inspected, questioned, improved where justified, tested, and validated with empirical evidence.**

## Status Key
- `[PENDING]`: Queued for inspection and questioning
- `[IN_PROGRESS]`: Currently under inspection, architectural questioning, or improvement
- `[IMPROVED]`: Improvements implemented and verified with component-level tests
- `[VALIDATED]`: Validated in real-world running IDE + whole-system regression passing (10/10 quality)
