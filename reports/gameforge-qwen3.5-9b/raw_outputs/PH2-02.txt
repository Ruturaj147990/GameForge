Here is the production-ready C++ implementation for the `ACharacter` pickup system. This solution adheres to Unreal Engine 5.7 conventions, utilizes `Enhanced Input` patterns (implied via RPC usage), ensures memory safety with `TObjectPtr`, and implements strict authority checks.

### Implementation Details

1.  **Client Method (`AttemptPickup`)**: Handles the local interaction trigger. It performs a basic sanity check and sends the RPC.
2.  **Server RPC (`Server_PickupItem`)**:
    *   **Reliability**: Declared as `Server` (default is reliable).
    *   **Validation**: Checks if the item is valid, owned by the player, and within interaction distance.
    *   **Authority Check**: Explicitly verifies `IsLocallyControlled()` to prevent cheating.
    *   **Logic**: Transfers ownership and adds the item to the inventory.
3.  **Header/Implementation Separation**: Follows standard UE project structure.
