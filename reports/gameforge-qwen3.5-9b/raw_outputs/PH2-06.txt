Implementing a robust `OnPlayerDeath` system requires strict adherence to the **Gameplay Ability System (GAS)** architecture. In a production-ready UE5 environment, we do not rely on raw C++ `OnTakeDamage` events or simple health float comparisons for death logic. Instead, we utilize **Gameplay Effects (GEs)** and **Gameplay Tags** to handle state transitions safely.

Below is the exact node sequence and logic flow for a Blueprint Actor (specifically a `Character` or `Pawn` inheriting from GAS) to handle damage, calculate health, determine death, and dispatch the event.

### Architecture Overview
1.  **Input**: `Event AnyDamage` (GAS Event).
2.  **State Calculation**: `Get Health` (via `Get Character` -> `Get Health`).
3.  **Logic Branch**: Compare Health vs. 0.
4.  **State Transition**: Apply `Gameplay Effect` (Damage Over Time or Death Effect) or manually set Health to 0 and apply `Gameplay Tag` (e.g., `IsDead`).
5.  **Dispatch**: Call `OnPlayerDeath` Event Dispatcher.
