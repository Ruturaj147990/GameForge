Here is a production-ready, memory-safe C++ implementation for Spherical Linear Interpolation (SLERP) within the Unreal Engine 5.7.4 ecosystem.

This implementation adheres to Unreal conventions:
- Uses `FQuat` (Unreal's quaternion type).
- Handles the "shortest path" logic by inverting the quaternion if the dot product is negative.
- Clamps the interpolation factor `t` to `[0, 1]`.
- Includes a fallback to standard linear interpolation (LERP) for very small angles to avoid numerical instability (singularity) when `t` is near 0 or 1.
